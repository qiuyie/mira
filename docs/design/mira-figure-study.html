Mira — direction B: the pin that came alive

A map pin is a teardrop stuck to one place. Turn it over, let it float, and it becomes a small light that travels with you. Her tip is her voice: it sways, perks, cocks, droops — and when she puts something on the map she flips tip-down and is, for a moment, a pin again.
